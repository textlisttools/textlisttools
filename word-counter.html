How the word and character counter works

Type or paste text into the box and every stat below it updates live: word count, total characters, characters excluding spaces, sentence count, and an estimated read time based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute.

It's built for situations with a hard limit — a tweet, a meta description, a college essay word count, or a video caption — where you need an exact, instant count rather than opening a full word processor.

Common uses

  • Checking essay or assignment word counts before submitting
  • Staying under character limits for social posts or ad copy
  • Estimating how long a script or speech will take to read aloud
  • Cleaning up text length for meta descriptions and titles

FAQ

How is read time calculated?
Using an average reading speed of 200 words per minute, rounded to the nearest second or minute.
How is a sentence counted?
The tool counts groups of text ending in a period, question mark, or exclamation point.
Is my text saved anywhere?
No — counting happens locally in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
