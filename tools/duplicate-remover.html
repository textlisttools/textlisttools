How the duplicate remover works

Paste your list, one entry per line, and press "Remove duplicates." The tool keeps the first time each line appears and deletes every repeat after it, so your original order stays intact.

Turn on "Ignore case" if entries like "Apples" and "apples" should count as the same item. Leave it off if capitalization matters for what you're comparing.

Common uses

  • Cleaning up an email list before a mail merge
  • Removing repeat entries from a giveaway or raffle list
  • De-duplicating tags, keywords, or category names
  • Tidying a copy-pasted list from a spreadsheet or PDF

FAQ

Does this remove blank lines too?
Yes — empty lines are stripped along with duplicates.
Which copy of a duplicate does it keep?
The first one it finds, top to bottom. Everything after it is removed.
Is my list saved anywhere?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
