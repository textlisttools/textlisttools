How the spinning wheel picker works

Paste your list, one entry per line, and press "Spin the wheel." The wheel splits into equal slices — one per item — and spins to a random stop under the pointer at the top. Whichever slice lands there is your winner.

Every item gets exactly the same odds regardless of slice size or position on the wheel. It's the same random pick as the list picker tool, just with an actual spin to watch play out.

Common uses

  • Picking a winner live during a raffle, stream, or classroom activity
  • Deciding what to eat, watch, or do next
  • Assigning a random task, chore, or turn order with a bit of suspense
  • Running a spin-to-win style giveaway

FAQ

Is every item equally likely to win?
Yes. Each entry gets an equal slice of the wheel and an equal chance, no matter how long the spin looks.
How many items can I add?
There's no hard limit, but wheels with more than 15–20 items get hard to read since the slices get thin.
Is my list saved anywhere?
No. The wheel is drawn and spun entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.
