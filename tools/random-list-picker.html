How the random list picker works

Type or paste your options into the box above, one per line, then press "Pick one." The tool uses your browser's random number generator to select a single item from the list with equal odds for every entry — no item is weighted more than another.

Nothing you type is uploaded or stored. The whole thing runs in your browser, so you can use it for anything from picking a winner out of raffle entries to deciding what to make for dinner.

Common uses

  • Choosing a winner from a list of giveaway or contest entries
  • Picking who goes first in a game or activity
  • Deciding between a shortlist of restaurants, movies, or names
  • Randomly assigning a task, prize, or role from a list

FAQ

Is every item equally likely to be picked?
Yes. Each line has the same chance of being selected, regardless of order.
Is there a limit to how many items I can add?
No hard limit — very long lists (thousands of lines) may just take a moment longer to process.
Does this save or share my list?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
